Candy Gulp – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Candy Gulp is made by Graph Vest ("we"). This policy explains what the game does with your information.

What we collect

Nothing. Candy Gulp has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your progress (levels, coins, boosters, lives, settings) is saved only on your device and never sent to us.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads and updates and may collect information under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

Candy Gulp is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds ads, analytics or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe exactly what is collected and why.
