Candy Gulp – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

Candy Gulp is made by Graph Vest ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and who handles it.

Your progress stays on your phone

Levels, coins, boosters, lives and settings are saved only on your device.

What the game sends

From version 0.1.7, to find levels that are too hard, balance coins and boosters, and fix crashes, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics and crash reports:

  • How you play: levels started, won, lost or quit; time, moves, boosters and continues in a level; coins and boosters earned and spent; tutorial steps; settings switched; when sessions start and end.
  • Your device and the app: device model, operating system, app version, language, connection type and, when the game crashes, the crash details and the device's state at that moment.
  • Random IDs: IDs our analytics tools create for each installation of the game, so they can count players and sessions. They aren't your name, email, phone number or advertising ID. Reinstalling the game, or clearing its data, gives new ones.
  • Approximate location: the country or city worked out from your IP address. The game never asks for or uses your precise location.

We don't collect your name, email, contacts, photos, precise location or advertising ID. There are no accounts, no ads and no purchases.

Remote settings

The game can receive settings from us, such as prices and rewards, so we can tune it without an update, and sometimes try two versions with different groups of players.

Who handles it

The data goes to GameAnalytics and to Google's Firebase (Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics and Remote Config). They process it on our behalf, on servers that may be outside your country, and send it encrypted. We don't sell your data, share it with anyone else, or use it for advertising.

How long it is kept

Google Analytics keeps it for 14 months after your last session, GameAnalytics for up to 12 months, and Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. After that it is deleted.

Your choices

The data is linked only to the random IDs above, not to you. To stop it, uninstall the game; to start fresh, clear its data or reinstall. Questions or requests: write to us below.

Children

Candy Gulp is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Google Play

Google Play handles downloads and updates under Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If a future version adds ads or purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released.
